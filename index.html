The evaluation contribution

  
Seven protocol choices, each read two ways

  
The paper's contribution is not a release but a protocol: it names seven choices
  an attribute-inference evaluation has to make, holds the released texts fixed, and re-reads them
  under the alternative. Six of the seven are re-read as verdicts over every pair of releases; the
  seventh is validated instead, and carries no sweep number. Select a row for what the choice is
  and what changing it did.

Who is asked about

  
The adversary can answer about the wrong person

  
In judgments naming several people, the adversary reads the first-named person
  almost perfectly and the others well below chance, because it answers about them from the
  first-named person's cues. Pooled, the two average to .53, which describes neither.

  

  
Sex AUC by subject position. An editor that moved the lower bar toward chance
  would look like it had increased discrimination.






  
The instrument

  
What the traceable editor contributes

  
The audit needs an editor whose every edit is attributable, so a counterexample
  can be built at precisely the sites another editor would redact. Each principle is removed one at
  a time, everything else held fixed.
